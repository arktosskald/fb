When to turn back

Set a return condition before leaving: no bandages, little compatible ammunition or a load that prevents retreat. Decide this at the campfire before valuable loot starts changing your judgement.

Save a supply list for a long trip and compare it with your backpack after the first fight. Stashed equipment will not help on another map; the laptop counts only carried items. An expensive artefact does not refill a magazine on the way home.

Declining a fight is a decision

Compare your remaining supplies with the return journey, not only the nearest enemy. One successful fight can leave you without treatment before the next transition. Taking three more orders does not bring the first reward closer. If a target disappears or becomes unavailable, check the order journal.
