The first hour beyond the perimeter

Do not fill your bag with everything you find. Check your weapon, compatible ammunition, bandages, treatment and water first. Choose a short route with a clear way back.

The aim of your first trip is to return with useful information. If a fight or an emission blocks the route, cancelling the trip costs less than losing your equipment. A known road is more useful than the promise of a rich stash.

The return route matters more than loot

Walk the first part of the route carefully: note shelter, exposed approaches and a place to avoid another squad. A cheap find does not pay for a route you cannot retrace in darkness. An unfamiliar sound is a reason to stop and look, not necessarily a reason to start shooting.
