A voice on an old tape

A man counts footsteps on the tape and asks whether it is recording. His daughter recognises the voice but neither the place nor the time.

She asks for original recordings: retelling loses background sounds, pauses and other voices. The tape is kept at home; verified details are entered in the diary.

If you have information

Separate a personal encounter from a retelling. Include the location, game date and details that can corroborate a sighting. A matching callsign alone does not establish identity.
