Writing a useful field report

Include the location, landmark, observation time and what you actually saw. “Heard growling by the bus” is more useful than claiming a chimera is certainly there. Distinguish another person’s story from your own observation.

Save important reports with their date. An old message may explain a risk without describing the current situation. F&B searches archived news and your notes; it does not continuously track every creature.

A report needs a source

Separate what you witnessed from what someone told you. Include the location, game time and a clear event description. “Heard gunfire near the warehouse” is more useful than an invented squad size. Do not publish exact coordinates for a stranger merely because an old post mentioned them.
