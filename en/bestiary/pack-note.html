Dog packs: watch the sides

One dog in your sights does not describe the whole pack. Find a retreat direction and check the flanks before stopping to fire.

Height can buy time to assess the situation, but it does not promise complete safety. Watch for other enemies and remaining rounds.

What to look for

One retreating mutant does not mean the encounter is over.

During an encounter

Check the flanks again after the first shot. A retreating target may draw you away from a safe route.

Check exact health, damage and resistances in your build. This entry describes behaviour and an approach to the encounter, without invented universal statistics.
